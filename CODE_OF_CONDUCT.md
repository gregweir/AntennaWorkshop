# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous community. Participation is open regardless of experience, background, identity, location, or disability.

## Expected Conduct

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and implementation choices without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and explain specialist radio, software, and regulatory terms.
- Cite primary sources and state uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations.
- Respect safety, privacy, licensing, attribution, and responsible disclosure requirements.
- Accept constructive review and correct mistakes transparently.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, doxing, and sustained disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Scope and Enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to [gweir@tartanleaf.com](mailto:gweir@tartanleaf.com). Include relevant links or records. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, reviewed fairly, and addressed in proportion to their severity and context.
